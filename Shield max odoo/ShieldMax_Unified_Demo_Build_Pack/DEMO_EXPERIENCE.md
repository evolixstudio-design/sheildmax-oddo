# Demo Experience — The “One Place” Story

The build should be optimized for a 4–6 minute live client presentation.

## Scene 1 — Public Storefront

Open `/`.

The first impression must be:

**This is not a Shopify template. This is Shield Max.**

Hero:
- strong Shield Max worksite imagery
- orange accent
- `Professional Safety & Protection Equipment`
- `Browse Products`
- `Request a Quote`

Show:
- categories
- certifications
- featured products
- brands
- project supply

Open the catalogue.

Point out:
- powerful filters
- stock/delivery
- certification data
- no prices

Add:
- Red Wing 95248 gloves × 40 pair
- Honeywell 7700 mask × 15 pcs
- Safety Vest Premium × 50 pcs

Open Quote List.

## Scene 2 — Submit a Website RFQ

Quote List should look premium and structured.

No monetary total.

Right summary:
- 3 products
- 105 total units
- Commercial Pricing: Prepared on Request
- Kuwait delivery
- Continue to RFQ

Submit prefilled demo business requirement.

Success state:
- `RFQ-2026-0157`
- expected follow-up message
- `Open Staff Control Center` demo shortcut

## Scene 3 — One Control Center

Open Control Center.

This is the “wow” reveal.

Header:
> Shield Max Control Center  
> Your website and business operations. One platform.

App launcher shows:
- Website
- RFQ Inbox with `1 New`
- CRM
- Quotations
- Sales
- Products
- Inventory
- Purchases
- Delivery
- Invoices
- Payments
- Finance
- Reports
- Settings

Notification tray also shows:
> New website RFQ — Kuwait Integrated Contracting W.L.L.

Open RFQ Inbox.

The newly submitted RFQ appears first.

## Scene 4 — Website Request Becomes ERP Work

Open RFQ.

Show:
- customer
- contact
- delivery
- products and quantities
- source `Website`
- timeline

Click:
`CREATE QUOTATION`

Quotation screen opens already filled with products.

Now public pricing becomes internal commercial pricing.

Enter/accept mock prices such as:
- 6.750 KWD
- 14.500 KWD
- 2.250 KWD

Totals update with 3-decimal precision.

Click:
`CONFIRM SALE`

Show connected confirmation:
- Sale created
- Inventory reserved
- Delivery order prepared
- Draft invoice created

This is the core value story.

## Scene 5 — Website Controlled From Same Platform

Return to launcher → Website.

Open Products.

Find `Safety Jogger - PROIMPACT`.

Open Website tab.

Toggle:
- Featured Product ON

Change delivery text:
- `Delivery in 2–4 days`
to
- `Available for same-day Kuwait dispatch`

Click:
`SAVE & PREVIEW`

Open Live Preview.

The storefront now reflects the change.

This proves:
> One product. One system. Website and ERP connected.

## Scene 6 — Close on “Everything in One Place”

Return to launcher.

End on all app cards.

Optional presenter line:

> “Your team will no longer manage a website here and business operations somewhere else. Shield Max becomes the website, the catalogue, the RFQ system and the ERP — all from one control center.”
