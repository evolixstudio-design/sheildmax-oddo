# Client Pitch Notes

Keep the presentation focused on business simplicity.

## Opening

> “Instead of keeping your website and ERP as two separate systems, Shield Max can become one platform. The website is simply the customer-facing side of the same product and business system.”

## When showing no prices

> “Because your prices move with quantity and market conditions, customers do not see a stale price. They build a requirement and request today’s quotation.”

## When submitting RFQ

> “This is not an email that somebody has to retype into the ERP.”

Submit.

## When Control Center shows the new RFQ

> “The website enquiry is already here — same products, same quantities, same customer requirement.”

## When converting to quotation

> “This is where your team adds today’s commercial price. The customer never sees an old fixed website price.”

## When showing Website Admin

> “The catalogue is not maintained twice. This is the same product. You control whether it appears on the website and what customers see.”

## Close

> “So the final system is not just a website replacement and not just an ERP. It becomes Shield Max’s own business platform — website, enquiries, sales and operations in one place.”
